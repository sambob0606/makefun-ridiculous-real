One sentence, 200 characters at most, pitching a bizarre startup idea that sounds ridiculous until you realise it would actually sell: we train dogs to perform in divorce court — because pet custody is the fight nobody settles, and the spouse a dog obeys in front of a judge is the one who keeps it.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the pitch to `/output/entry.txt` (override with `OUTPUT_DIR` for local runs, e.g. `OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/out python3 main.py`).
